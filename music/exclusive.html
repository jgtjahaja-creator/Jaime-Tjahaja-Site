Journal

"Indeed I Do" was written during an arts residency in Ubud, Bali, where I'd been spending my days exploring the local music scene — open mics, informal ensembles, learning from musicians I'd just met.

Then I received news that a close friend had passed away unexpectedly. I picked up my guitar, and the song just poured out. I remember the strange sensation of singing the melody and playing at the same time — it felt like witnessing myself from the outside, as if the song already existed and I was simply the first to hear it.

Back in California, I recorded it with a talented group of musicians, including a special vocal performance by Maya Elise (Maya Elise and The Good Dream).

"Indeed I Do" is out now as a single alongside a music video by Bay Area filmmaker Edgar Lara.
