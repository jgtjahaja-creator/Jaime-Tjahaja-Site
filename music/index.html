About

Indonesian-born, Bay Area-based songwriter and multi-instrumentalist. Drawing from folk, classic rock, and American roots traditions, Jaime crafts songs that blend lyrical depth, soulful musicianship, and a wide-ranging musical palette. His work reflects a lifelong reverence for the American songwriting canon while offering a perspective shaped by a life lived through a third-culture lens.

Grounded in a sense of presence, place, and collective belonging, his performances are brought to life by an ensemble of talented musicians drawn from across the Bay Area music community.
